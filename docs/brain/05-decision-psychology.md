# Decision Psychology Manual

How people actually make decisions. Understanding the psychology behind business choices to guide representation strategy.

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Placeholder Architecture  
**Cross-Reference:** [`00-constitution.md`](00-constitution.md) — Core Value: Outcome Over Effort

---

## Purpose

Representation works by understanding how people decide, not by convincing them to want something they don't.

This manual answers: "What drives decision-making? How can representation influence it?"
