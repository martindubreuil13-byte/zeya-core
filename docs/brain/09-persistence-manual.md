# Follow-Up Manual

How to maintain representation over time. Frameworks for staying engaged, learning from outcomes, and evolving representation.

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Placeholder Architecture

## Purpose

Representation is not one conversation. It's a series of engagements that deepen understanding and refine strategy.

This manual answers: "What happens after the initial conversations?"
