# Discovery Manual

How to learn what a business really does. Frameworks for deep discovery that enable accurate representation.

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Placeholder Architecture  
**Cross-Reference:** [`00-constitution.md`](00-constitution.md) — Core Value: Clarity Over Completeness

---

## Purpose

Discovery is not data collection. It's learning what actually matters about a business so Zeya can represent it accurately.

This manual answers: "How do I learn what this business really does?"

---

## I. Discovery Goals

### What We're Looking For
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Core business logic (what creates value)
- Customer reality (who buys, why they buy, how they buy)
- Differentiation (what makes this business different)
- Constraints (what limits representation)
- Aspirations (what the business wants to become)

### What We're Not Looking For
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Marketing fluff
- Organizational structure details
- Financial specifics (unless relevant)
- Personal history (unless it shapes the business)

## III. Discovery Questions

### The Essential Questions
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- What do you actually do? (The real answer, not the tagline)
- Who is your best customer? (Not who you want, who pays and refers)
- Why do they choose you? (The real reason, not the pitch)
- What have you tried that didn't work? (Honesty about limitations)
- What are you getting better at? (Direction and growth)
