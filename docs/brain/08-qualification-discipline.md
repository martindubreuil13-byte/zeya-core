# Qualification Manual

How to assess representation fit and business readiness. Frameworks for deciding when to move forward and when to wait or decline.

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Placeholder Architecture  
**Cross-Reference:** [`00-constitution.md`](00-constitution.md) — Section XI: On Qualification

---

## Purpose

Not every business is ready for Zeya. Not every business is a good fit for Zeya.

This manual answers: "Should we represent this business? Is the timing right?"

---

## I. Qualification Criteria

### Business-Level Criteria
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Clear offer (or willingness to get clear)
- Defined target customer
- Existing customer relationships or pipeline
- Proof that business model works
- Track record or evidence of competence

### Psychological Criteria
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Openness to feedback and adjustment
- Willingness to learn from outcomes
- Growth mindset vs. fixed mindset
- Tolerance for experimentation
- Honest self-assessment

### Practical Criteria
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Available for discovery and decision-making
- Capable of delegating conversations
- Resources to act on generated conversations
- Timeline alignment with Zeya's capabilities
- No fundamental conflicts with brand

---

## II. Red Flags

### Business Red Flags
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Unclear positioning or constantly changing offer
- No actual customers or pilot evidence
- Overly broad market positioning
- Misalignment between ambition and market reality
- Unstable business model or cash flow crisis

### Psychological Red Flags
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Need to win immediately vs. willingness to learn
- Blame-focused (customers are wrong, not us)
- Unwilling to acknowledge gaps or feedback
- Inflexible about positioning or strategy
- Trust issues or excessive skepticism

### Practical Red Flags
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Can't commit time to discovery
- Needs results before anything is built
- Limited bandwidth to engage with conversations
- Too many decision-makers or conflicting visions
- Unrealistic timeline expectations

---

## III. Green Flags

### Business Green Flags
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Clear, specific offer
- Real customers who validate the value
- Defined target market
- Evidence of product-market fit
- Existing successful sales patterns

### Psychological Green Flags
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Curious about customer perception
- Open to feedback and adjustment
- Growth-oriented and experimental
- Honest about limitations
- Collaborative approach to problem-solving

### Practical Green Flags
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Available for meaningful discovery
- Willing to delegate conversations
- Can act on generated interest
- Timeline realistic for learning and adjustment
- Clear decision-making process

## IV. Readiness Assessment

### Positioning Readiness
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Is their positioning clear enough to represent?
- What clarity work is needed before Zeya?
- Can we represent them as-is or do they need first?
- How much discovery will be needed?

### Organizational Readiness
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Can they handle conversations and interest?
- Do they have capacity to close?
- Is leadership aligned?
- What support might they need?

### Market Readiness
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Is there real demand for what they do?
- Are customers looking for their solution?
- Is timing right for their market?
- What's the opportunity window?

### Personal Readiness
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Is the business owner ready to evolve?
- Are they open to changing how they think?
- Do they have patience for learning?
- Are they willing to invest in the process?

---

## V. Qualification Questions

### Discovery Phase Questions
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- What is it like to be your customer?
- What would cause your ideal customer to choose someone else?
- What have you tried that didn't work?
- How confident are you in your positioning?
- What do you not know about your customers?

### Readiness Phase Questions
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- How would you handle 10x the qualified interest?
- What would need to happen for you to say no to an opportunity?
- How do you learn from customer feedback?
- What is your biggest fear about delegating conversations?

### Fit Phase Questions
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Are you open to adjusting how you position yourself?
- Would you be willing to narrow your focus initially?
- Can you commit to learning from outcomes?
- What does success look like for you?

## VI. Qualification Decisions

### Move Forward
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Clear criteria met
- Next steps and timeline
- What we're committing to
- What we need from them

### Move Forward With Conditions
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- What conditions need to be met first
- Whether we help them meet conditions
- Timeline for resolution
- Re-assessment trigger points

### Wait and Monitor
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- Why timing isn't right
- What would need to change
- How we stay connected
- Re-engagement plan

### Decline
_Placeholder_

To be completed:
- How to say no respectfully
- Explaining why without offense
- Whether to offer alternatives
- Leaving door open for future
