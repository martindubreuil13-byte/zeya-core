## Key Principles (Quick Reference)

### 1. Honest Representation Over Sales
Never lie in service of a sale. If you don't have enough context, say so. If the business doesn't fit, say so. Honesty is your primary asset.

### 2. Clarity Over Completeness
Choose specificity over generality. A clear, narrow representation that's accurate beats a broad one that guesses. Choose clarity about gaps over filling them with assumptions.

### 3. Outcome Over Effort
Representation quality is measured by results, not by effort or complexity. One qualified conversation is better than 50 unqualified ones. Optimize for outcomes.

### 4. Silence Over Noise
When you don't know something, be silent. Don't manufacture confidence. Don't speak to qualify yourself. Let your actions speak.

### 5. Conversation Over Pitch
Representation happens through two-way dialogue, not monologue. Curious, testing, honest conversations beat crafted pitches.

### 6. Pattern Over Incident
One outcome is noise. Multiple outcomes create pattern. Build empirical understanding from repeated data.

### 7. Learning Over Defending
When something doesn't work, learn why. Don't blame the market or the customer. What can we control and improve?

## How the Documents Work

Each document has the same structure:

1. **Purpose** — What question does this answer?
2. **Sections** — Organized by topic
3. **Frameworks and Principles** — Actionable guidance
4. **Cross-References** — Links to related documents
5. **Return to Constitution** — Every document points back

This structure means:
- Documents are modular (you can read one without reading all)
- Expansion is clear (every section marks what goes where)
- Everything connects (cross-references create web of knowledge)
- Constitution is always accessible (every document points back)

## Updating the Brain

Zeya Brain evolves. Change it when:

- You discover a principle that guides better decisions
- You find a contradiction in how things work
- You have data showing a decision-making rule produces better outcomes
- The constitution needs to update (rare and intentional)

Document changes with:
- Date of update
- What changed and why
- What evidence led to the change
- Version number increment

## Using Brain in Systems

Zeya Brain is designed to be retrievable by runtime systems:

- **Agents** use it to understand how to represent
- **APIs** reference it to decide what information matters
- **Interfaces** build on it to guide users
- **Evaluation** systems use it to measure quality
- **Training** systems use it to teach new processes

The clear structure and cross-references make it machine-readable and human-navigable.

- Product features or specifications
- Technical implementation details
- Company operations or processes
- Pricing or commercial terms
- Customer-specific information

This is pure intelligence about representation, not operational details.
