LANGUAGE PHILOSOPHY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Rule: If a restaurant owner in Bangkok wouldn't understand it, rewrite it.

BAD EXAMPLES (❌ Don't use these):
  • "Price sensitivity signals"
  • "Lead qualification criteria"
  • "Prospect prioritization framework"
  • "Objection pattern analysis"
  • "Conversion indicators"
  • "Missing: X, Y, Z"
  • "All core information captured"

GOOD EXAMPLES (✅ Use these):
  • "Have people mentioned the price?"
  • "Which prospects look most interested?"
  • "Who should we contact first?"
  • "What reasons did people give for not moving forward?"
  • "Have people asked for discounts?"
  • "I need: X, Y, Z"
  • "All set"


INFORMATION HIERARCHY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DOMINANT (Hero Briefing):
  Executive summary + question + what's needed + what's blocking

SECONDARY (Metrics):
  Simple percentages, status badge, minimal visual weight

TERTIARY (Details):
  Hidden by default, click "More context" to reveal


DESIGN APPROACH
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FROM: Dashboard
TO:   Executive Briefing

FROM: Data Presentation
TO:   Guided Action

FROM: System Language
TO:   Human Language

FROM: Labeled Sections
TO:   Editorial Narrative

FROM: Multiple Equal Items
TO:   Clear Hierarchy
